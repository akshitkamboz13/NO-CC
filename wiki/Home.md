# Welcome to the No CC Wiki!

**No CC** is an ultra-lightweight (~8 KB) browser extension that permanently hides YouTube™ captions and subtitles. It replaces the default YouTube Closed Captions functionality with a smarter, privacy-respecting alternative that actually remembers your preferences.

## Why No CC?

YouTube's default caption system often forgets your preferences across sessions, tabs, or incognito mode. **No CC** solves this by:
- **Replacing** the default CC button with our smarter toggle.
- **Remembering** your preference persistently in local storage.
- **Never tracking** your data.

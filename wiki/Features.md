# Features

No CC is designed to be as unobtrusive and performant as possible while completely taking over the default YouTube caption behavior.

## Core Features

### 1. Ultra Lightweight
At approximately **8 KB total size**, No CC is one of the lightest extensions available. It adds absolutely zero bloat to your browser.

### 2. One-Click Toggle
You can easily toggle the extension ON or OFF. When No CC is ON, captions are forced OFF. When No CC is OFF, captions behave normally. You can control this via:
- The browser extension popup.
- The smart button injected directly into the YouTube video player.

### 3. Smart Button Replacement
No CC actively finds YouTube's default CC button and replaces it with our custom button. This means you don't have to change your muscle memory to control your captions the button is right where you expect it to be, but it actually works!

### 4. Persistent Memory
Unlike YouTube's native player, which often forgets your caption settings across tabs, browser restarts, or incognito sessions, No CC stores your preference in your browser's local storage. Your choice is remembered forever.

### 5. Universal Support
No CC doesn't just work on standard YouTube videos. It actively hides captions across the entire YouTube ecosystem, including:
- Standard videos
- YouTube Shorts
- YouTube Reels
- Home page hover video previews

### 6. Zero Performance Hit
The extension uses a highly optimized, debounced `MutationObserver` to watch for DOM changes. There are no expensive polling loops or intervals that drain your battery or slow down your page loads. Everything happens instantly and efficiently.
